## Technical SEO and AEO Strategy, Built for How Search Works

Search has two layers now. The traditional results page still matters. But a growing share of discovery happens through [AI tools that skip the link list](/blog/how-ai-search-engines-decide-what-to-cite-and-how-to-get-on-the-list/) entirely and synthesize a direct answer. If your content isn't structured for both, you're invisible to a chunk of your audience.

My work covers both layers:

**Technical SEO** Site architecture, crawl efficiency, Core Web Vitals, structured data and schema markup, hreflang and international SEO, XML sitemaps, and large-scale content strategy. This is the foundation that makes everything else work.

**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** [So what is AEO?](/blog/what-is-aeo-and-geo-business-owners-guide-ai-search/) Optimizing content to be cited and surfaced by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews. This means content structure, entity recognition, FAQ and schema implementation, and building topical authority that AI systems treat as trustworthy.

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** Positioning a brand as a recognized, citable source in AI-generated summaries. Longer-term than AEO, and increasingly, where brand visibility is won or lost.
