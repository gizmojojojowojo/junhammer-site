## [Healthcare Site Migration](/work/healthcare-website-consolidation/)

A healthcare device manufacturer operating five separate websites faced a fragmented digital presence. Traffic was spread thin, rankings were inconsistent, and conversions were suffering. We consolidated all five properties into a single, unified WordPress site without sacrificing the organic visibility each site had built independently.

The migration involved complex redirect mapping, link equity consolidation, and e-commerce URL architecture across a large product catalog. The result: a 60% increase in traffic to the main site and a 130% increase in conversions as consolidated authority replaced scattered performance. Rankings improved alongside a brand presence that finally matched the company's market position.
