#### Client Overview

Client:

Citrus Spine Clinic

Industry:

Healthcare/Spine Clinic

Location:

Florida

Challenge:

New website with minimal traffic, competing against established healthcare giants.

#### Objective

To increase organic traffic and establish Citrus Spine Clinic as a thought leader in spine health by leveraging a robust SEO strategy focused on Google's EAT (Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) and YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) principles.

#### Strategy

1. **Content Strategy:**
  - Developed a content plan focusing on thought leadership in spine health.
  - Created in-depth, authoritative blog posts, articles, and guides addressing common spine-related issues.
  - Updated main service pages with comprehensive, user-friendly content.
2. **On-Page SEO:**
  - Optimized meta tags, headers, and internal linking structures.
  - Ensured all content adhered to EAT and YMYL guidelines, emphasizing the expertise of the clinic's professionals.
3. **Technical SEO:**
  - Improved site speed and mobile responsiveness.
  - Implemented structured data markup to enhance search engine understanding of the site's content.
4. **Link Building:**
  - Secured high-quality backlinks from reputable healthcare and wellness websites.
  - Engaged in local SEO efforts, including directory listings and local citations.

#### Implementation

- Conducted thorough keyword research to identify high-value, spine-related terms.
- Regularly published high-quality content and monitored performance using Google Search Console (GSC) and analytics tools.
- Engaged with the local community through events and partnerships to build brand authority.

#### Results

- **Total Clicks:** Increased from 2.74K (1/1/22 - 3/31/22) to 39.4K (1/1/23 - 3/31/23).
- **Total Impressions:** Grew from 339K (1/1/22 - 3/31/22) to 2.25M (1/1/23 - 3/31/23).
- **Keyword Rankings:** Outranked major healthcare institutions like the Cleveland Clinic and Johns Hopkins for several spine-related terms.

#### Conclusion

Citrus Spine Clinic significantly increased its organic traffic and established itself as a trusted authority in spine health through a strategic SEO campaign focused on content quality, technical optimization, and adherence to Google's EAT and YMYL principles. The impressive growth in clicks and impressions, as evidenced by the GSC data, underscores the success of the campaign in driving visibility and engagement.

This case study highlights the importance of a well-rounded SEO strategy, especially for new websites in competitive industries like healthcare. Citrus Spine Clinic achieved remarkable results by focusing on quality content and technical excellence, surpassing even the most established competitors.

While traffic alone is not the goal, this increase still illustrates the power of SEO-informed content.
