## Search now has two layers

The results page still matters. But a growing share of questions get answered directly by AI assistants that summarize a few sources and skip the list of links. If your content is not structured for that layer, a chunk of your audience never sees you, even when you rank.

I have worked on AEO since before most agencies had a name for it, and I [write about it regularly](/blog/topic/aeo/). I have led AEO and technical SEO for enterprise health systems, where the bar for trust is higher than almost anywhere else, and I now lead AEO for nonprofit and social-impact organizations.

## What an AEO program covers

**Visibility baseline.** We pick the questions your buyers or patients actually ask and check which brands each AI assistant cites today.

**Content structure.** Pages get rebuilt around direct, self-contained answers, question-based headings and the [40-word paragraph rule](/blog/the-40-word-rule-how-to-structure-paragraphs-for-ai-citations-in-2026/) so AI systems can extract them cleanly.

**Entities and schema.** Consistent organization, person, service and medical schema, plus the off-site signals that tell AI systems who you are.

**Technical access.** AI crawler rules, server-rendered content, llms.txt and page speed, the [technical foundation every GEO strategy needs](/blog/the-technical-seo-foundation-every-geo-strategy-needs-to-succeed/).

**Reporting.** Citation share across assistants, tracked monthly next to your SEO numbers.

## AEO consultant, GEO consultant or AI SEO consultant?

They are three names for overlapping work. AEO focuses on getting your content pulled into answers for specific questions. GEO, [generative engine optimization](/blog/generative-engine-optimization/), focuses on how AI systems describe and recommend your brand overall.
