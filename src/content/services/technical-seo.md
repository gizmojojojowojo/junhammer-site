## The foundation everything else sits on

Content and AEO work only pay off if search engines and AI crawlers can reach, render and understand your site. Technical SEO is that foundation: architecture, crawl paths, speed, structured data and clean signals about which page is the right answer.

## Migrations without the traffic cliff

Migrations are where sites lose years of work in a weekend. My process:

1. **Inventory.** Crawl every URL and pull 16 months of Search Console data so we know what earns traffic and links.
2. **Decide.** Keep, merge, refresh or retire each URL, with the data in front of us.
3. **Map.** One-to-one 301 redirects to the most relevant new page, never a blanket redirect to the home page.
4. **Test.** Staging crawls, redirect checks and schema validation before launch.
5. **Monitor.** Daily Search Console checks for the first weeks after launch, with fixes the same day.

I used this process to [consolidate five healthcare device sites into one](/work/healthcare-website-consolidation/) and to rebuild the site you are reading now.
