## Strategy before tactics

Good SEO starts with what people are actually trying to do when they search. I build every program on [search intent research](/blog/user-intent-completet-guide-understanding-search-intent-seo-success/), then decide what to create, what to fix and what to cut.

That approach grew a Florida [spine clinic's search clicks more than 14 times in a year](/work/citrus-spine-clinic-seo/), to the point it outranked national health systems for core terms. It worked because the content was specific, credible and written for patients, not because of tricks.

## What I handle

- **Research.** Keywords, intent, competitors and the gaps they leave open.
- **Content.** Topic clusters, briefs and pages that earn authority instead of chasing algorithm updates.
- **On-page.** Titles, structure, internal linking and schema on every priority page.
- **Local and international.** Google Business Profile, citations, hreflang and regional content.
- **Reporting.** Numbers tied to leads and revenue, explained in plain language.

## SEO that holds up in AI search

Everything I build for classic SEO is also structured for [answer engines](/services/answer-engine-optimization/). The same habits that win citations in AI answers, such as direct answers, clear structure and credible sourcing, also tend to lift rankings.
