## Key takeaways

- Published 2026 rate cards put independent AI consultants at $150 to $350 an hour, boutique firms at $250 to $500, and Big 4 or strategy firms at $350 to $500 or more ([Leanware](https://leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost), [Ryan Doser](https://ryandoser.com/ai-consultant-cost/)).
- Freelance marketplaces run far lower. Fiverr lists hourly rates for AI strategy consultants from $10 to $125 ([Fiverr, 2026](https://www.fiverr.com/resources/guides/costs/ai-expert)).
- Small fixed projects, such as an AI strategy assessment, commonly fall between $5,000 and $25,000 ([Leanware](https://leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost)).
- Monthly advisory retainers start around $2,000 to $5,000 for 5 to 10 hours a month ([Leanware](https://leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost)).
- AI development work is priced differently. Clutch reviews put a typical AI development project at $10,000 to $49,999 ([Clutch, 2026](https://clutch.co/developers/artificial-intelligence/pricing)).
- In Denver, the first AI firms listed on Clutch's directory show rates from $50 to $199 an hour, with minimum projects from $5,000 to $100,000 or more ([Clutch Denver](https://clutch.co/consulting/ai/denver)).
- The rate cards tie price to scope, seniority, firm type and whether the consultant also builds the system.

AI consultant cost depends on three things: who you hire, how the work is priced, and what you need at the end. A freelance prompt expert, an independent senior consultant and a Big 4 team all call themselves AI consultants. Their rates differ by a factor of ten or more.

This guide pulls the published 2026 ranges into one place, explains what moves the price, and shows what a sensible first engagement looks like. Every figure links to its source. I don't list my own rates here.

## How much does an AI consultant cost?

Independent AI consultants typically charge $150 to $350 an hour in published 2026 rate cards. Fixed projects start around $5,000, and monthly retainers start around $2,000 ([Leanware](https://leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost), [Ryan Doser](https://ryandoser.com/ai-consultant-cost/)).

Those figures come from consultants and agencies that sell the work. Neither source publishes the data behind its bands, so read them as market signals. Marketplace data, where it exists, runs lower.

## What drives the price of AI consulting?

Five factors set most of the price: seniority, firm type, pricing model, scope, and whether the consultant also builds what they recommend.

- **Seniority.** Leanware's bands roughly triple from junior to senior consultants ([Leanware](https://leanware.co/insights/how-much-does-an-ai-consultant-cost)).
- **Firm type.** Orient Software gives an example: a solo senior freelancer at $350 an hour, and an equivalent consultant from a top agency billed at $450 to $550 ([Orient Software](https://www.orientsoftware.com/blog/ai-consultant-hourly-rate/)).
- **Pricing model.** Hourly suits open questions. A fixed fee suits a defined deliverable such as an opportunity map. A retainer suits ongoing advice after a plan exists.
- **Scope.** One team's workflows cost far less to map than a company-wide program.
- **Strategy only, or strategy plus build.** A consultant who can also build turns a recommendation into a working pilot. A strategy-only engagement leaves you to hire a developer next.

## What should a first AI consulting engagement look like?

A good first engagement is small, fixed in scope and ends with one working pilot or a ranked plan. It should answer where AI saves time in your business before anyone signs a long retainer.

A typical sequence:

1. **Discovery.** Interviews with the people doing the work, plus a review of your tools, data and constraints.
2. **Opportunity map.** A ranked list of AI use cases with effort, risk and payoff.
3. **Pilot.** One workflow set up end to end, with prompts, review steps and a way to measure results.
4. **Roll-out plan.** Guardrails, training and a backlog, including anything worth building as a custom tool.

Ask any consultant you are vetting what you will have at the end of step 2 and step 3. A clear answer is a good sign. For a broader roadmap, see [how to become an AI-native company](/blog/how-to-become-ai-native/).

## FAQ

### How much does an AI consultant charge per hour?

Published 2026 rate cards put independent AI consultants at $150 to $350 an hour, boutique firms at $250 to $500, and large strategy firms at $350 to $500 or more. Marketplace freelancers charge less.

### How much does a small business pay for AI consulting?

A small, fixed-scope AI strategy assessment commonly costs $5,000 to $25,000. Monthly advisory retainers of 5 to 10 hours start around $2,000 to $5,000.

### Is an AI consultant worth the cost?

It depends on the time the work saves. Start with a small fixed-scope project that ends in a ranked plan or a working pilot, then decide on more spend from measured results.

### What is the difference between hourly, project and retainer pricing?

Hourly pricing suits open questions. Project pricing suits a defined deliverable, such as an opportunity map. A retainer suits ongoing advice once a plan exists.

### Do AI consultants in Denver charge less than national firms?

No published data compares them. Clutch's Denver listings show firms from $50 to $199 an hour, which overlaps the national ranges. Compare scope and seniority rather than location.

### What does an AI consultant actually do?

An AI consultant looks at how your team works, finds tasks where AI saves real time or improves quality, and turns that into a plan: tools, builds, rules and how to measure results.
