**Key takeaways**

- An **AI SEO agency** supplies a team that produces the work. An **AI SEO consultant** supplies senior judgment and usually directs others on execution.
- The deciding question is who will write, publish and do the outreach. If nobody on your side can, you need an agency's capacity. If someone can, a consultant is often enough.
- [MarketerHire's 2026 hiring guide](https://marketerhire.com/blog/answer-engine-optimization-consultant) lists fractional AEO consultant retainers at $3,000 to $15,000 a month, project engagements at $8,000 to $25,000, and agency retainers at $10,000 to $35,000 or more.
- AI search raises the stakes on getting the strategy right. [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) found Google users clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, versus 15% without one.
- A hybrid works well: a consultant owns the plan and measurement, and an agency or freelancers produce content.
- Whichever you pick, ask for an AI visibility baseline in the first month.

If you are deciding between an AI SEO agency and an AI SEO consultant, start with your own team, not the provider's pitch. The right choice depends on which parts of the work you can already cover. This guide breaks AI SEO into its parts, shows who usually does each, and gives you a simple way to decide.

## What is the difference between an AI SEO agency and an AI SEO consultant?

An AI SEO agency is a team that plans and produces the work. An AI SEO consultant is usually one senior specialist who sets the strategy, does the expert work and directs your people or vendors on the rest.

"AI SEO" is the umbrella term many buyers search for. It covers traditional SEO plus [answer engine optimization](/services/answer-engine-optimization/) and [generative engine optimization](/blog/generative-engine-optimization/): getting your business mentioned and cited in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.

| | AI SEO agency | AI SEO consultant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who you work with | An account manager plus a team | The specialist directly |
| Strongest at | Volume: content, outreach, page production | Judgment: strategy, audits, technical fixes, prioritization |
| Who executes | The agency | Usually your team, your agency or freelancers |
| Typical engagement | Monthly retainer | Project, audit or fractional retainer |
| Best fit | Teams without writing or publishing capacity | Teams with capacity that need direction |
| Main risk | Output that looks busy but does not move AI visibility | A good plan nobody has time to carry out |

## What does AI SEO work actually include?

AI SEO work falls into four parts. Knowing which parts you can cover tells you what to hire.

1. **Strategy and measurement.** Choosing the questions that matter, recording where you appear in AI answers today, and reporting on change.
2. **Technical foundation.** Crawl access, indexing, rendering, site structure and structured data. AI systems still find most sources through search indexes, so [technical SEO](/services/technical-seo/) comes first.
3. **Content.** Rewriting key pages so they answer questions directly, and publishing new pages where you have gaps.
4. **Off-site presence.** Accurate listings and mentions on the directories, review sites and articles that AI assistants cite.

In a typical split, a consultant leads parts 1 and 2 and directs parts 3 and 4, while an agency covers all four with most of its staff on parts 3 and 4.

## How much do AI SEO agencies and consultants cost?

Market ranges vary widely by scope. In its [2026 guide to hiring an AEO consultant](https://marketerhire.com/blog/answer-engine-optimization-consultant), MarketerHire lists these typical costs:

| Engagement type | Typical cost (MarketerHire, 2026) |
| --- | --- |
| Fractional AEO consultant retainer | $3,000 to $15,000 per month |
| Project engagement (audit and build) | $8,000 to $25,000 total |
| AEO agency retainer | $10,000 to $35,000+ per month |
| In-house senior AEO lead | $140,000 to $220,000 base salary |

MarketerHire notes that scope and brand-authority level drive cost more than seniority alone.

The top-line number can mislead. A consultant at a lower monthly rate still needs someone to carry out the content work. Add the cost of that execution, whether it is your staff's time or freelancers, before you compare.

## When should you hire an AI SEO agency?

Hire an agency when you need a steady volume of production and have nobody to do it. Signs that point to an agency:

- You need several new or rewritten pages every month and have no writer.
- Nobody on your team can publish to the website without help.
- You want outreach, digital PR or listing management handled for you.
- You prefer one vendor to manage, even if you pay for coordination.

When you evaluate agencies, ask them to [show the work behind the dashboard](/blog/ai-seo-agency-show-work/) on one of your real pages.

## When should you hire an AI SEO consultant?

Hire a consultant when you have execution capacity but lack senior direction. Signs that point to a consultant:

- You have a marketer, writer or existing agency who can carry out changes.
- You are not sure what to fix first, or your current agency's work is not moving results.
- You need technical work done well: migrations, structured data, crawl issues.
- You want the strategy and the measurement owned by someone with no incentive to sell more production hours.

A consultant can also audit an agency's plan before you sign, or review its work once you have. For how to vet one, see [what to look for when hiring an AEO consultant](/blog/hiring-an-aeo-consultant-work-evidence-look-for/).

## A simple test: who will do the work?

Answer these four questions:

1. **Can someone on your side write or edit web pages each month?**
2. **Can someone publish changes to your site without a developer ticket that takes weeks?**
3. **Is there already an agency or freelancer producing content for you?**
4. **Does anyone own measurement and reporting today?**

If you answered no to 1 and 2, start with an agency. If you answered yes to 1 or 3, start with a consultant. If you answered no to 4, whoever you hire must set up AI visibility tracking in the first month.

## What should either one deliver in the first 90 days?

Expect the same core outputs from either model:

- **A baseline.** Where you appear, or do not, in AI answers for your priority questions, and which competitors and sources show up instead.
- **A prioritized fix list.** Technical, content and off-site work in order of impact.
- **Shipped fixes.** The first round of changes live on your site, not only in a document.
- **Readable reporting.** A monthly view of citations and mentions next to your Search Console data.

If a provider cannot describe how it will measure AI visibility, keep looking. See [how AI search engines decide what to cite](/blog/how-ai-search-engines-decide-what-to-cite-and-how-to-get-on-the-list/) for what that measurement should track.
