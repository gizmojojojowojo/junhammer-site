## The challenge

Citrus Spine Clinic launched a new website with almost no search traffic, competing for spine-health questions against Cleveland Clinic, Johns Hopkins and other national health systems with decades of authority.

## The approach

Health content falls under Google's "Your Money or Your Life" standards, so the strategy started with trust: Expertise, Experience, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T).

**Content strategy.** A thought-leadership plan built around the questions patients actually ask about spine conditions and treatments. In-depth guides and articles replaced thin service copy, and core service pages were rewritten to be comprehensive and readable.

**On-page SEO.** Titles, headings and internal linking were optimized across the site, and every piece of content highlighted the clinic's medical expertise.

**Technical SEO.** Faster pages, better mobile performance and structured data that helped search engines understand the clinic, its physicians and its services.

**Authority and local signals.** Links from reputable healthcare and wellness sites, plus directory listings and local citations.

## The results

Comparing Q1 2022 with Q1 2023 in Google Search Console:

- **Clicks** grew from 2.74K to 39.4K.
- **Impressions** grew from 339K to 2.25M.
- **Rankings:** the clinic outranked Cleveland Clinic and Johns Hopkins for several spine-related terms.
- **Leads** grew 300% over the year, and monthly clicks went from about 200 to more than 22,000.

## Why it matters now

Traffic alone is not the goal, but this project showed that a small, credible publisher can beat giants when the content is specific and trustworthy.
