  - q: "What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?"
    a: "Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of structuring content so that AI-powered systems \u2014 such as Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and voice assistants \u2014 can easily extract, understand, and cite your information when users ask questions. Unlike traditional SEO, which focuses on ranking pages in a list of results, AEO focuses on becoming the direct answer. This requires clear content structure, authoritative sourcing, schema markup, and a deep understanding of how large language models retrieve and synthesize information. As more users bypass traditional search results in favor of AI-generated answers, AEO is becoming essential for maintaining visibility."
  - q: "What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?"
    a: "Generative Engine Optimization is a strategic framework for ensuring your brand appears in AI-generated search results and recommendations. Generative AI systems like ChatGPT, Google's Search Generative Experience, and Perplexity don't just return links \u2014 they synthesize answers from multiple sources and decide which brands to mention by name. GEO involves optimizing your digital footprint so these systems recognize your brand as authoritative, relevant, and trustworthy. This includes content strategy, entity optimization, reputation signals, and technical implementation that positions your organization to be cited rather than overlooked."
  - q: "How is SEO for AI different from traditional SEO?"
    a: "Traditional SEO focuses on ranking web pages in search engine results through keyword optimization, backlinks, and technical performance. SEO for AI goes further by optimizing how your content is understood and cited by large language models. AI systems don't just match keywords \u2014 they evaluate context, authority, and entity relationships to determine which sources to trust and reference. This means your content needs clear structure, consistent entity signals across the web, authoritative citations, and formatting that AI can parse efficiently. Organizations that adapt now will dominate visibility as AI-driven search becomes the default experience."
