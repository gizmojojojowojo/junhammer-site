## Why AI search matters for local businesses

AI summaries now sit above many search results. A [Pew Research Center analysis](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) of March 2025 browsing data found that Google users who saw an AI summary clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits, compared with 15% of visits without one. If your business is not named in the summary, fewer people reach your site at all. [Generative engine optimization](/blog/generative-engine-optimization/) is how you fix that.
