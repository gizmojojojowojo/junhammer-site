**Why AEO requires a different strategy than SEO** The mental model shift that changes how you approach every piece of content you publish.

**How AI engines decide what to cite** The four factors that determine whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI pulls from your site or your competitor's.

**Technical setup that actually matters** Schema markup, llms.txt, crawlability for AI bots — what to implement, what to skip, and why.

**How to write content that gets pulled into AI answers** Specific structural techniques that make your content easier for AI engines to extract and use.

**Building topical authority AI engines recognize** Why a focused, consistently published site beats a high-domain-authority generalist every time.
